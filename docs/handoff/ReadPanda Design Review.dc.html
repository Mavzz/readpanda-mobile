1ReadPanda — design review

Recreated from Mavzz/readpanda-mobile@master, reviewed against your own DESIGN.md ("Nocturnal Sanctuary") and the product goal: a private, asynchronous book club for close friends.

Context & assumptions

  • All screens rebuilt from source (src/screens, src/components, src/styles/global.js) with mock data; covers are placeholders.
  • Recreations use the system font — faithfully: the app never loads Manrope, though DESIGN.md mandates it.
  • Skipped not_in_use/ and Favorites (dead code).

The headline finding

The visual system is strong and mostly well-applied — but the product's core promise (reading together with friends, comments that unlock as you reach them) has zero UI anywhere. Three of four tabs are placeholder scaffolding, and two of them (Join Room / My Rooms) are near-duplicates.

Top fixes, in order

  • Merge Join Room + My Rooms → one Rooms tab (4 tabs → 3).
  • Put the current read + friends' activity on Home.
  • Load Manrope; kill zero-filled stats & fake data.
  • Sweep the DESIGN.md violations flagged below (1px borders, dividers, sub-24 radii, off-palette #ccc).

Review summary

Grouped by the concerns you flagged.

System adherence

  • Manrope is mandated but never loaded — the whole app runs on system fonts.
  • Border violations: dashed create-card, notification left-accent, Create-Bucket input + selection outline, Profile dividers, badge stroke.
  • radius.sm (12) used on 5+ elements despite the "never below md" rule; meanwhile 48px on small cards over-rounds. Suggest a mobile scale: 16 / 24 / 28 / full.
  • Off-palette grays: #ccc (3 screens), #555, #fff icons.

Navigation & IA

  • Join Room + My Rooms are one tab pretending to be two. Merge → Home · Reading · Rooms.
  • "Explore Books" tab actually holds the whole library + buckets — name it Home or Library.
  • Profile route bug: navigate('InterestScreen') vs registered 'Interest'.
  • Search is visible but alerts "not implemented" — hide it until real.

Contrast & accessibility

  • Body pairs are strong (#dae2fd / #d6c3b2 on navy ≈ 9–11:1). Good foundation.
  • Weak spots: badge text 12px at ~4:1, metadata at 0.7 opacity ≈ 3.4:1, 11px tab labels.
  • Placeholder-only form fields lose their labels once filled.
  • Long-press to delete a bucket is undiscoverable — add an explicit affordance.

The social gap

  • Nothing in the UI shows a friend, a room, or a comment — the reason the app exists.
  • Async-first framing is the differentiator: show where friends are in the book and comments waiting ahead, not chat.
  • Stores exist (roomStore, messageStore, readingProgressStore) — the redesigns below give them a surface.

Redesign directions — moderate evolution

Same Nocturnal Sanctuary system, correctly applied (Manrope loaded, no borders, tonal layering, one primary button) — and the social core made visible. IA: Home · Reading · Rooms.
